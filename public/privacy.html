Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

SmartCB is a cash-book app that runs in your browser. It is operated by the developer of this site. Contact: muqeednse@gmail.com.

What we access

When you sign in with Google, SmartCB asks for your name, email, profile photo, and permission to use Google Drive files that you create in SmartCB or explicitly choose with Google Picker. The app does not request access to your whole Drive.

How data is used

  • Your Google profile is used only to show which account is signed in.
  • Cash-book entries are stored in your browser on this device and written back to the Excel workbook or Google Sheet you opened.
  • The app does not run a SmartCB server that stores your ledgers.

Sharing

Ledger data is sent to Google Drive using Google’s APIs so it can be saved in your account. It is not sold. It is not shared with other companies for advertising.

Retention

Local copies stay in this browser until you clear site data. Drive copies stay in your Google account until you delete them. You can disconnect SmartCB in your Google Account permissions.
